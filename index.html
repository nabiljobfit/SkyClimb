24/7 Support

How do I book an open trip?

Simply navigate to our Open Trip section, choose your destination, and click "Book Seat". You can proceed with payment via bank transfer or credit card. Confirmation is instant.

Is gear rental included in the price?

Standard packages include basic logistics (tents, cooking gear). Personal gear like jackets, sleeping bags, and carriers can be rented separately through our Rental section.

What if the weather is bad?

Safety is our priority. If BMKG declares unsafe conditions, we will reschedule the trip or offer a full refund minus administration fees, depending on the specific trip policy.

Do you have trips for beginners?

Absolutely! Look for the "Beginner Friendly" tag in our Open Trip filter. These mountains have easier trails and lower elevations suitable for first-timers.

Can I request a private guide?

Yes. For private expeditions or customized itineraries, please use the Collaboration/Contact form to request a private certified guide.
